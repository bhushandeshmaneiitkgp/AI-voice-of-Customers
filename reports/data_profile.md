## 7. Known limitations of this dataset

- **Complaint-biased sample.** Not representative of the user base. Every downstream claim must be scoped *within this review dataset*.
- **Platform rating gaps are confounded** with how each platform's reviews were collected. Cross-platform *satisfaction* ranking is not supported and is deliberately not offered by the product.
- **Three usable trend buckets.** No seasonality or year-over-year.
- **No identifiers in source.** No user ID (repeat reviewers are invisible), no app version, city, or order value — so root-cause output is limited to *hypotheses* from text, never confirmed causes.
- **App-store-shaped feedback.** Reviews mix app bugs with operational failures and cannot be tied to specific orders or SKUs.
