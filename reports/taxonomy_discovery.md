## Robustness checks

**Near-duplicate inflation.** Comparing all rows against representative-only rows, the largest shift in any area share is **0.40 pp** (`wallet_and_credits`). Templated reviews are not driving the taxonomy.


**Unmatched residual.** **411 reviews (8.9%)** match no area probe, with a mean rating of **2.73** vs **1.83** for the corpus. The residual skews short and positive. Manual reading found it dominated by generic sentiment ("worst app ever") and by lexical recall misses ("received one item less" is fulfilment but matches no probe) — not by a missing category. This is the main reason these estimates are floors.


## Caveats carried from Phase 1

- **rating imbalance** — 77.5% of reviews are negative. Area frequencies describe a complaint sample, not a customer base. Never state an area's share as "% of customers affected".

- **temporal concentration** — 87% of reviews fall in Oct-Dec 2024; Blinkit has 12 reviews before October and Zepto has 1. Area trends are only comparable across platforms within that three-month window.

- **truncation bias** — 370 reviews (8.0%) hit a 500-character scraper cap. Truncated reviews average 1.36 stars vs 1.83 overall and are 90.8% negative vs 77.6% -- they are cut before any resolution is described. Severity and support_escalation must be treated as lower-confidence on these.

- **near duplicate templates** — 18 near-duplicate groups covering 49 reviews. Inflation of area shares is under 0.4 percentage points, so the taxonomy is not built on templated noise -- but pain-point counts should still be reported both raw and representative-only.

- **non representative sample** — App-store reviews are self-selected. Cross-platform differences in area share are differences within this dataset, never statements about which platform's customers are better served.

- **discovery estimates are lower bounds** — All discovery_estimate_pct values come from keyword probes. Manual reading of unmatched reviews showed most are recall misses (e.g. "received one item less" is order_fulfilment but matches no probe). Treat these as floors and expect LLM classification to assign more.
